About this site

This is a curated list of software-development links Seth finds interesting. Raindrop is the capture inbox; the GitHub repository is the durable publishing layer.

Goose prepares concise notes in a casual colleague-to-colleague voice, with enough context to remember why a link seemed worth saving. Publication happens by pull-request review, so Seth stays in the loop before links go live.

Source links belong to their original authors. This site is just a pointer, a short note, and a trail of what caught Seth's eye.
