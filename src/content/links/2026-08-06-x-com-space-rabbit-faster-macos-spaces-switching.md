Space Rabbit is a tiny open-source macOS menu bar app that removes the slow Spaces-switching animation by triggering a faster-than-human swipe gesture. If you live in multiple Spaces all day, this is one of those small workflow fixes that can feel weirdly high-leverage: less waiting, less context-switch drag, and no hunting through hidden system settings for a toggle Apple does not expose.
