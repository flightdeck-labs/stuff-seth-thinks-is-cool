summary: "OpenDisplay (peetzweg, ~5.3k stars, GPL-3.0) turns an iPhone, iPad, or spare Mac into a real extended display for your Mac — not a mirror. USB or Wi‑Fi, Retina-sharp, touch/scroll back into macOS, no account, no dongle, no subscription. Sidecar is iPad-only and same-Apple-ID; Duet went subscription; Luna wants hardware. This is the self-hosted option: two small apps, one TCP hop, screen never leaves your devices.\n\nMac sender is macOS 14+ (signed/notarized DMG, not App Store — private virtual-display APIs). iOS/iPadOS receiver via TestFlight or source; spare-Mac receiver is a separate app on macOS 12+. Latest noted release: 1.25.0 (2026-10-01).\n\nWhy it matters: if you’ve got a spare Apple screen sitting around, this is the free Sidecar/Duet alternative that actually extends the desktop."
