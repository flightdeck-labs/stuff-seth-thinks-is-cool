Types are meant to follow data → scale → mark the whole way; SVG is the default renderer with Canvas as a separate import; motion, compact/D3 scales, and custom marks are opt-in so you don’t pay for unused pieces. 1.0 is the “we’ll support this API” line.\n\nWhy it matters: if a product chart is going to grow annotations, mixed marks, and app-specific tooltips, this is the TanStack-shaped alternative to outgrowing Chart.js / react-charts wrappers."
