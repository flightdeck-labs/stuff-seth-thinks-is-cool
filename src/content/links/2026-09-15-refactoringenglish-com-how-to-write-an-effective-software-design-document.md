Michael Lynch’s design-doc playbook from Google/Microsoft: write one when the work is multi-person, long-lived, or expensive to reverse; skip cheap UI/implementation choices that you can fix in an afternoon. The useful part is the section checklist — objective, goals/non-goals, scenarios, diagrams, SLOs, interfaces, security/privacy, open issues, alternatives — plus a public example for his Little Moments app and a follow-on on getting review without stalling.

Why it matters: a practical filter for *whether* a design doc is worth writing and *which* decisions belong in it, not another generic “document everything” lecture.
