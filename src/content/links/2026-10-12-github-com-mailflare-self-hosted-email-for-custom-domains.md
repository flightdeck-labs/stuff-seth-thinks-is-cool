Per domain you can send/receive with Cloudflare Email Routing, Resend, or Amazon SES.\n\nShared and personal mailboxes, routing rules, calendar + public booking pages, AI assistant, and MCP keys with separate permissions. Receiving via Cloudflare Email Routing is free; sending can stay on Resend/SES free tiers.\n\nWhy it matters: a serious “Gmail without Google” for people already on Cloudflare, with a busy ~4.9k-star repo and real team features (shared inboxes, booking, MCP)."
