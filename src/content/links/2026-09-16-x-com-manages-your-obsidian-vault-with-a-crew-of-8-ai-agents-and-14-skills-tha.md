Tom Dörr pointed at `gnekt/My-Brain-Is-Full-Crew`: an MIT-licensed multi-agent Obsidian setup (8 agents + 14 skills) where chat is the interface. You dump, they file, search, transcribe meetings, and triage email/calendar. Same crew across Claude Code, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, and Codex CLI. Pitch is for people drowning in notes and life admin, not vault tinkerers; conservative by default (archive, don’t delete). Token cost and “the vault becomes theirs” are the obvious caveats.

Why it matters: a useful comparison point for multi-agent inbox/triage setups — especially the dispatcher + specialized skills split versus one agent doing everything.
